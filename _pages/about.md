I am an engineer, researcher, and technical leader based in Shanghai, studying how optical performance translates into what people actually see and experience through AR glasses. My work brings together XR optics, human visual perception, and AR+AI to connect laboratory measurements with how wearable displays are used in everyday environments.

As Technical Manager at Instrument Systems, Konica Minolta Group, I lead XR research and technical development in China. I built our Shanghai XR optical laboratory, established the local engineering team, and have partnered with more than 20 global device makers and display manufacturers—including Apple, Samsung, and BOE—to define how next-generation near-eye displays are evaluated and tested from lab to production. Across optical measurement, visual perception, and computational modeling, my goal is to establish evaluation frameworks that align emerging display technologies with the realities of human vision.

Building on this optical and perceptual foundation, I also explore the frontier of AR+AI and autonomous engineering systems. I investigate how see-through AR glasses serve as wearable interfaces for AI—linking real-world AI use cases directly to optical and visual design requirements, and independently prototyping first-person applications that augment human perception and memory. Alongside wearable applications, I develop agentic, hardware-in-the-loop platforms where AI orchestrates physical optical instruments and software to automate experimental discovery.

I am the lead author of the *XR Optical Testing Handbook* (ISBN: 978-7-89569-660-0), have published more than 20 peer-reviewed articles, and regularly deliver invited talks at international forums including SID, ICDT, and CIOE. I serve as a Technical Committee Member of the Society for Information Display (SID) China and a Voting Expert Member of SID’s International Committee for Display Metrology (ICDM), contributing to international display measurement methods and standards.

My academic background spans mechanical engineering at the University of Toronto (BS) and Tsinghua University (MS), followed by a PhD in Software Engineering at Tongji University, where I focused on artificial intelligence.
