I am an engineer and researcher based in Shanghai, working at the intersection of XR optics, visual perception, and AI. As Technical Manager at Instrument Systems, Konica Minolta Group, I lead XR research and technical development, working with global R&D teams, device makers, and optical suppliers.

My research connects optical measurements with what people see through AR glasses. I study how optics, ambient light, and gaze affect visual performance, and how these factors shape the design of AI glasses. My broader interests include computational imaging and AI tools for engineering.

I contribute to display metrology through the International Committee for Display Metrology (ICDM) and SID China. I hold a PhD in Software Engineering from Tongji University, an MS in Mechanical Engineering from Tsinghua University, and a BS in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Toronto.
