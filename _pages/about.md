I am an engineer and researcher based in Shanghai, studying how optical performance translates into what people see through AR glasses. My work brings together **XR optics, visual perception, and AI** to connect optical measurements with the experience of using these devices in everyday environments.

As **Technical Manager** at Instrument Systems, Konica Minolta Group, I lead XR research and technical development in collaboration with global R&D teams, device makers, and optical suppliers. I built our Shanghai XR optical laboratory and established the local engineering team. My research spans optical measurement, visual perception, and computational methods, with a focus on understanding and improving the visual performance of emerging display technologies. My broader interests include computational imaging and AI tools for engineering.

I am the lead author of the *XR Optical Testing Handbook* (ISBN: 978-7-89569-660-0) and have published more than 20 peer-reviewed articles. I serve as a Technical Committee Member of the Society for Information Display (SID) China and a Voting Expert Member of SID’s International Committee for Display Metrology (ICDM), contributing to display measurement methods and standards.

My academic background spans mechanical engineering at the University of Toronto and Tsinghua University, followed by a PhD in Software Engineering at Tongji University, where I focused on AI.
