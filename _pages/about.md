**AI + Optics Expert | XR Innovator**. <br><br>
My work focuses on the intersection of AI and optics, with the core mission of building the next generation of human-computer interaction. As an Application Manager at Instrument Systems, a Konica Minolta company, I lead the technological fusion of AI and optics, dedicated to developing core technologies for next-generation XR devices. My background also includes developing specialized application solutions for industry leaders like Apple and Samsung.<br>
My academic journey began in Mechanical Engineering (B.Eng., University of Toronto; M.S., Tsinghua University). Years of industrial practice gave me insight into the revolutionary potential of AI for traditional engineering fields, which drove me to pursue and obtain a full-time PhD in AI from Tongji University while advancing my career. This experience has given me a unique perspective spanning from hardware to algorithms, allowing me to directly apply cutting-edge theory to real-world industrial challenges. I believe XR, and AR in particular, is the ultimate platform to overcome current AI bottlenecks, creating the ideal architecture for the next generation of context-aware intelligent collaborative partners—a vision I am now dedicated to realizing.

**AI+光学专家 | XR技术创新者**<br><br>
我的工作聚焦于人工智能与光学的交叉领域，核心使命是构筑下一代人机交互体验。作为 Instrument Systems (柯尼卡美能达集团旗下公司) 的应用经理，我主导着AI与光学的技术融合，致力于为下一代XR设备开发核心技术，并为苹果、三星等行业领导者开发了专业的应用解决方案。<br>
