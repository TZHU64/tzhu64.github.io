朱天行 Tianxing Zhu

应用经理 @ 柯尼卡美能达 (世界500强) | 人工智能博士
Application Manager @ Konica Minolta (Fortune 500) | PhD in AI

AI + Optics Expert | XR Innovator.

My work focuses on the intersection of AI and optics, with the core mission of building the next generation of human-computer interaction. As an Application Manager at Instrument Systems, a Konica Minolta company, I lead the technological fusion of AI and optics, dedicated to developing core technologies for next-generation XR devices. My background also includes developing specialized application solutions for industry leaders like Apple and Samsung.
My academic journey began in Mechanical Engineering (B.Eng., University of Toronto; M.S., Tsinghua University). Years of industrial practice gave me insight into the revolutionary potential of AI for traditional engineering fields, which drove me to pursue and obtain a full-time PhD in AI from Tongji University while advancing my career. This experience has given me a unique perspective spanning from hardware to algorithms, allowing me to directly apply cutting-edge theory to real-world industrial challenges. I believe XR, and AR in particular, is the ultimate platform to overcome current AI bottlenecks, creating the ideal architecture for the next generation of context-aware intelligent collaborative partners—a vision I am now dedicated to realizing.

AI+光学专家 | XR技术创新者

我的工作聚焦于人工智能与光学的交叉领域，核心使命是构筑下一代人机交互体验。作为 Instrument Systems (柯尼卡美能达集团旗下公司) 的应用经理，我主导着AI与光学的技术融合，致力于为下一代XR设备开发核心技术，并为苹果、三星等行业领导者开发了专业的应用解决方案。
我的学术道路始于机械工程（本科：多伦多大学；硕士：清华大学）。多年的产业实践让我洞察到人工智能对传统工程领域的革命性潜力，这驱使我在职攻读并获得了同济大学的全日制人工智能博士学位。这段经历赋予我从硬件到算法的独特视角，让我能够将前沿理论直接应用于真实的工业挑战。我认为XR（特别是AR）是突破当前AI交互瓶颈的终极平台，为构建下一代具备情境感知能力的智能协作伙伴提供了理想架构——推动这一愿景成为现实，也正是我当前的使命。

Mar 23, 2025 I was honored to represent Instrument Systems as an invited speaker at the 2025 International Conference on Display Technology (ICDT 2025), where I presented our research titled Measurement of Near-Eye Displays Using a Goniophotometer and Imaging Light Measurement Device.
Sep 02, 2024 I authored the book Handbook of VR Optical Measurement. The book is divided into nine chapters, comprehensively covering international standards, measurement equipment, measurement methods, and real-world case studies. It systematically explains how to use optical measurement technology to precisely measure augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and mixed reality (MR) devices and their components. This book aims to help interested people gain a deeper understanding of XR technologies, while standardizing related terminology and technical knowledge to provide strong support for industry development. In addition to the physical book, I have also published its core content on Github.
