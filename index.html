朱天行 Tianxing Zhu

应用经理 @ Instrument Systems（柯尼卡美能达集团）| 同济大学人工智能博士
Application Manager @ Instrument Systems (Konica Minolta Group) | PhD in AI from Tongji University.

I’m Tianxing Zhu, an AI + Optical Engineer and an XR Innovator.

I am currently the Application Manager and AI Development Leader at Instrument Systems. My work focuses on bridging the gap between optics and AI to deliver next-generation XR experiences. Previously, I held the role of Senior Application Engineer at Instrument Systems, where I led the development of new application and optimized automated workflows—both internally and for key clients such as Apple, Samsung, and BOE.
Recognizing the transformative potential of AI in the optical industry, I pursued my PhD in AI at Tongji University (2020-2025) while working part-time as the Senior Application Engineer at Instrument Systems. During this period,I dedicated myself on unlocking the synergies between AI and optics. As AI Development Leader, I proposed and led several interdisciplinary projects, including AI-powered Mura defect detection and AI-enhanced optical knowledge base system, driving both technological advancements and practical applications.
Today, I view XR (particularly AR) as the ideal platform for AI, serving as the critical interface for its next evolution. Current AI systems face significant input-output bottlenecks. For instance, state-of-the-art language models like GPT-4o rely on text-based interactions limited to 50 tokens per second—a bandwidth representing just 1.3% of natural human conversation. AR devices, with their multimodal input, mixed-reality output, and edge computing capabilities, align seamlessly with AI’s architectural requirements. This makes AR glasses the perfect platform for next-generation AI agents.

朱天行 | AI+光学工程师 · XR技术创新者

我目前担任Instrument Systems的应用经理兼AI开发负责人，致力于探索光学与人工智能的深度融合。在此之前，我曾任Instrument Systems的高级应用工程师，主导新应用开发与自动化流程优化工作，即包含内部项目，也包括苹果、三星和京东方等关键客户。
在意识到人工智能对光学行业的变革潜力后，我选择在同济大学攻读AI方向的博士学位（2020-2025）。与此同时，我兼职担任Instrument Systems的高级应用工程师，专注于挖掘AI与光学之间的协同效应。我提出了并主导了多个跨学科项目，例如基于AI的Mura缺陷检测和AI增强光学知识库平台，推动了技术进步与实际应用的结合。
基于技术演进逻辑，我认为XR（尤其是AR）将成为AI发展的理想平台，是其下一阶段演进的关键接口。当前的AI系统面临显著的输入输出瓶颈：例如，最先进的语言模型如GPT-4o依赖于文本交互，每秒仅能处理50个token，这一带宽仅相当于自然人类对话速度的1.3%。而AR设备凭借多模态输入、混合现实输出以及边缘计算能力，完美契合了AI架构的需求，使AR眼镜成为下一代AI技术的绝佳载体。
