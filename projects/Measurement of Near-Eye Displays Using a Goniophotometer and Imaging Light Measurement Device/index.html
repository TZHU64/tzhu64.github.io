Measurement of NED Using a Goniophotometer and Imaging Light Measurement Device

2025 International Conference on Display Technology (ICDT 2025)

There is a critical need for reliable characterization and adjustment of near-eye displays (NEDs) used in AR/VR headsets, both during development and production. In the development phase, highly accurate measurements are essential to optimize NED quality, while in production, speed becomes paramount, though results must remain comparable to the precise measurements obtained during development.

This study compares two measurement devices with distinct working principles for color and luminance evaluation of an NED. For lab use, the DMS goniophotometer—which mimics the human eye and delivers highly accurate spectral measurements—is employed. However, this device requires several hours to complete a full measurement. In contrast, for production environments, the LumiTop imaging colorimeter, equipped with an array spectroradiometer and a wide field-of-view lens, is utilized. The LumiTop can measure the entire field of view of an NED in seconds while maintaining spectroradiometric accuracy.

The comparison focuses on measurement results from both devices using a test NED consisting of a pancake lens paired with an LCD display. The findings reveal the inherent trade-offs between accuracy and speed, demonstrating the LumiTop system’s potential to meet the demands of high-throughput production without compromising comparability to the high-accuracy DMS results.
