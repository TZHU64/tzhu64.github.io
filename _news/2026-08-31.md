I was honored to be invited to speak at the [2026 SID Display Week Review Workshop](https://sidchina.cn/col.jsp?id=717) in Qingdao on August 31, where I presented “Advances in XR Visual Perception Quality and Evaluation.” My talk reviewed advances highlighted at Display Week 2026 and shared insights from my own XR research, including my understanding of how optical performance, human visual perception, and real-world viewing conditions shape XR visual quality and its evaluation.
