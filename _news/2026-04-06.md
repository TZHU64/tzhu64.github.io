I was honored to represent Instrument Systems at the 2026 International Conference on Display Technology (ICDT 2026), where I presented our three recently published papers advancing AR optics and near-eye display measurement. Our presented research includes [Gaze vs. View: A Framework for Correlating Dynamic and Static Measurements in AR Optics](https://www.instrumentsystems.com/en/news-portal/current-lectures/details/gaze-vs-view-a-framework-for-correlating-dynamic-and-static-measurements-in-ar-optics), [Colorimetric Characterization of See-Through Near-Eye Displays](https://www.instrumentsystems.com/en/news-portal/current-lectures/details/colorimetric-characterization-of-see-through-near-eye-displays), and [The Impact of Waveguide Spectral Filtering on AR Color Fidelity](https://www.instrumentsystems.com/en/news-portal/current-lectures/details/the-impact-of-waveguide-spectral-filtering-on-ar-color-fidelity).
