I was honored to represent Instrument Systems at the 2026 International Conference on Display Technology (ICDT 2026), where I presented our three recently published papers advancing AR optics and near-eye display measurement. Our presented research includes Gaze vs. View: A Framework for Correlating Dynamic and Static Measurements in AR Optics, Colorimetric Characterization of See-Through Near-Eye Displays, and The Impact of Waveguide Spectral Filtering on AR Color Fidelity.
